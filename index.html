About me

I'm Dr Brain Pennie

I'm Dr Brian Pennie. I’m a neuroscientist, keynote speaker, author, podcast host, and behaviour change specialist. My work combines neuroscience, lived experience, and practical tools to help people and organisations create meaningful, lasting change.
After overcoming a 15-year heroin addiction, I rebuilt my life, completed a PhD in neuroscience, lectured in Trinity College Dublin, founded an award-winning healthcare communications agency, and now work with leaders, teams, schools, and organisations across Ireland and beyond.
Through keynotes, workshops, coaching, and consulting, I help people understand the science of change… and apply it in real life.
Because no matter where you start, change is possible.

MY story

I was a Heroin Addict

One autumn day in October 2013, my life changed forever. By then, I had been a heroin addict for more than 15 years and it was slowly killing me. That day, in what seemed like an instant, the world seemed to glow. Captivated by this sudden change, I started to question why I felt so alive. I didn’t see it at first, but then it hit me. It had tortured me my entire life. The voices in my head, the stories that I spun myself – the ones that drove my anxiety and, in turn, my addiction. They were gone. For as long as I could remember, thinking had consumed me – my mind racing about what I needed to do, tirelessly working out ways to escape my bodily agitations. But now, the voices were silent. My anxiety was gone. The race was over and my new life had begun.

A second chance

Bonus Time

This sudden shift in perspective set me off on an amazing journey, and with a newfound love of learning, I went to university to study the human mind. I had so many questions: What is a perspective shift? Why did I feel so alive? What is the relationship between anxiety and the voice in our heads? Why do people suffer? How can I help others escape their suffering?

Life had gifted me a second chance, but after so many years of addiction, I had no idea how to live in the real world. With that, I began to develop what I called a ‘Programmefor Life,’ a framework that would help me to master my mental, emotional, and external world. It worked, helpingme to become a doctor of psychology, a neuroscientist,a published author, a lecturer at the top two universitiesin Ireland, and a coach to some of Ireland’s most influential leaders.

Most importantly, I now have a beautiful relationship with anxiety, helping me to live life in an energetic, emboldened, and carefree way.

A new life

A New Purpose

Now that I’d conquered my own struggles, I wanted to share my programme with others who might need it.

I began speaking in schools, and after one talk in 2016, a 14-year-old kid approached me. With incredible enthusiasm and energy, he told me “I’m going to do this. I’m going to do everything you said.” This sparked something visceral inside of me. I’d never felt so alive. With a fire in my belly and a mission to share what I’d learned, I began refining my Programme for Life, creating my own business called Change is Possible. Through this company, I deliver workshops and keynote talks to some of the world’s largest organisations. But I never forgot my roots, and I provide mentorship programmes, talks, and workshops to schools and prisons around Ireland.

Design

Next, we design a plan of action with you to address the key concerns. This may include a tailored workshop, a once-off talk, or a series of in-depth coaching sessions.

Engage

We then engage with your team and put the plan into action, ensuring the strategies are practical, relevant and immediately useful.

Adapt

Finally, we adapt and learn. Once the new approach has been applied, we help refine and fine-tune the process so your team is better equipped to handle similar challenges in the future

BEYOND RESILIENCE

Course & Module Details

BIOLOGICAL MODULE

Find out how to apply the latest neuro and science-based techniques to both tackle chronic and real-time stress

Module 1 will dissect the interactive nature of the brain, body, and mind. With a focus on neuroplasticity, polyvagal theory, our immune systems, and how our bodies react to internal and external triggers, you'll learn how to apply the latest neuro and science-based techniques to tackle both chronic and real-time stress and anxiety. You will also learn about the relationship between the brain with both exercise and nutrition, as well as the neuroscientific underpinnings of my personal 3-min morning routine, called BAG, which harnesses the power of Breath work, Affirmations, and Gratitude.

Module 1 will teach you how to

  • Build a science-based toolbox to navigate stress and anxiety
  • Develop a growth mindset
  • Turn obstacles into opportunities for learning and growth

EMOTIONAL MODULE

Understand how to use and create space during emotionally challenging events.

Module 2 will focus on emotional resilience, with an emphasis on learning how to pause and create space during emotionally challenging events. This workshop will also highlight the dangers of trying to control what you cannot control, as well as the chain-reactive nature of emotional triggering to uncontrollable events. You will also learn how to avoid emotional burnout by preventing emotional hijackings before they take hold.

Module 2 will teach you how to

  • Stay calm under pressure
  • Catch emotional hijackings in full flight
  • Avoid emotional burnout

PSYCHOLOGICAL MODULE

Learn how to change your relationship with negative self-talk and limiting beliefs.

Module 3 will focus on our thoughts, beliefs, and self-talk. These are all huge forces that shape how we respond to external events – which can either empower us to overcome challenges or hold us back from reaching our full potential. Drawing on psychological concepts such as growth mindset and antifragility, you will learn how to develop greater confidence in yourself and lean into adversity using obstacles as fuel for growth.

Module 3 will teach you how to

  • Bounce back from setbacks
  • Utilise visualisation techniques to develop mental toughness
  • Build a wall of evidence to increase confidence and self-belief

SOCIAL MODULE

Discover how to create boundaries around challenging relationships, and surround yourself with people who will bring out the best in you.

Module 4 focuses on our relationships with others, which can have a profound impact on our emotional well-being and resilience. With an emphasis on nurturing good relationships and removing problematic relationships (both personal and professional), this workshop will highlight the latest research from polyvagal theory, and my recent work with the godfather of this theory, Professor Steven Porges.

Module 4 will teach you how to

  • Learn how to say no to things that don't serve you
  • Implement personal boundaries
  • Nurture and enhance existing relationships

PROGRAMME FOR LIFE

Course & Module Details

1. LOST POTENTIAL

Discover how negative thinking and limiting beliefs hold us back in life and learn about the power of self-talk and its impact on our emotional worlds

Module one focuses on a problem many people face today. Namely, limiting beliefs, negative thinking, and the stories we tell ourselves. This foundational human problem is why many people do fail to live up to their true potential, restricting themselves through self-doubt and a harsh inner critic.

Module 1 will teach you how to

  • Discover how negative thinking and limiting beliefs hold us back in life
  • Learn about the power of self-talk and its dark relationship with emotion
  • Connect more deeply and learn more about work colleagues

2. BREAKING FREE

Find out how to foster greater self-belief and confidence by building a wall of evidence that focuses on people's unique talents

Module two will address many of the human challenges highlighted in session one. It focuses on building self-awareness, dissolving challenging emotions, creating a space between external stressors and our emotional response to them, and reframing limiting beliefs using a powerful technique from cognitive behavioural therapy.

Module 2 will teach you how to

  • Develop greater self-awareness around the mental and emotional worlds
  • Learn how to reframe limiting beliefs (e.g. I'm not good enough)
  • Build greater self-confidence while unearthing your own personal superpower

3. THE MIND-BODY CONNECT

Learn how our brain and body react to both calming and challenging situations.

Module three helps people to understand the mind-body connection, in terms of both neuroscience and psychology. With an emphasis on a stressed-versus-relaxed state of being, this session will highlight our amazing capacity to create change from the inside out.

Module 3 will teach you how to

  • Understand how our brain and body reacts to both calming and challenging situations
  • Build a science-based toolbox that will change your relationship with anxiety and stress
  • Become more focused, present, and attentive by utilising the neuroscience of mindfulness

4. A GUIDANCE SYSTEM FOR LIFE

Identify and develop a values-based framework that will help you make decisions and take actions that align with your True North.

Module four will highlight the immense power of creating a values-based system to direct your life. Our values provide a pathway for finding our true authentic selves, as well as guiding us to what's important in our lives.

Module 4 will teach you how to

  • Develop a core set of values that will guide decisions and actions
  • Discover the difference between value-based and feelings-based decisions
  • Learn how to use a simple and practical metaphorical tool to guide you in times of stress

5. MAGIC AND MIRACLES

Push towards purposeful action, while at the same time finding balance in daily life.

Module five will discuss the topic of purpose, pointing people towards their "why" and true calling in life – both personally and professionally. This process requires action, and knowing how to take those first steps is crucial.

Module 5 will teach you how to

  • Shine a brighter light on your why, your purpose, and true calling in life
  • Prevent burnout and feeling overwhelmed by creating a better work/life balance
  • Overcome procrastination and fear and take purposeful action in both work and life

6. TAKING ACTION

Change behaviour and bad habits, and learn how to create and sustain progressive habits, including powerful morning routines, intention-setting and more

Based on the latest research from behavioural psychology, module six will show people how to change their behaviour and habits. We will discuss the science of habits, the power of keystone habits, how to break bad habits, and how to create and sustain progressive habits.

Module 6 will teach you how to

  • Break bad habits and develop good habits using neuro and behavioural science
  • Create powerful morning routines that will positively flow into other areas of their lives
  • Become more intentional, self-directed, and self-accountable for their mental, emotional, and physical wellbeing
